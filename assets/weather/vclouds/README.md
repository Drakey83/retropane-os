# VClouds weather icons

The default weather icon pack of RetroPane, **by VClouds**
([deviantart.com/vclouds](https://www.deviantart.com/vclouds)). These are his
icons, unchanged: they are **not RetroPane's own work and not under
RetroPane's licence**. They stay in this folder under his terms.

**Credit:** icons by **VClouds**, https://www.deviantart.com/vclouds . The
About screen and every place these pictures are shown or shipped carry this
credit and the link, as he asked.

## His terms

From the Read Me in his icon download (kept here unchanged as
`Read Me (from the icon download).txt`): free for non-commercial use; credit
him and link back to his page; not for commercial use without his permission.

## Permission for RetroPane

The project owner (Reid Carlisle) wrote to VClouds on 2026-10-12, describing
RetroPane as a free, open-source hobby operating system for the Dell Axim
X51v, with no ads, no sales and no paid versions, and offering: credit to him
(his name and a link to his DeviantArt page) in the About screen and beside
the icon files; the icons kept in their own folder under his terms, apart from
the code's licence; and no use of the icons outside RetroPane. He replied on
2026-10-16 that the project may use his icons and skin design as long as the
credit is given as offered. The exchange is kept in the owner's DeviantArt
messages.

What that covers: using the icons in RetroPane, the open-source,
non-commercial project described. **Selling RetroPane, or a device with it,
is not covered:** anyone who does that asks him first. Using the icons
outside RetroPane is not covered either.
