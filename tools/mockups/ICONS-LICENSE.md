masters of RetroPane's base icon set) and every picture made from them is
licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
licence (CC BY 4.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Credit it as: "Icons from the RetroPane project, CC BY 4.0".

Chosen by the project owner, 2026-10-30 (design/icons.md, vote IC1). The
rest of RetroPane, including the code in these files that draws the
pictures, stays under the project's licence (GPL-2.0-or-later, `LICENSE`).

Not covered: the SD and CF card icons (Juanje's, `assets/cards/README.md`)
and the weather icons (VClouds', `assets/weather/vclouds/README.md`), which
keep their own terms. The Flash storage icon is drawn to match the cards'
style but is RetroPane's own drawing, under CC BY 4.0 like the rest.
