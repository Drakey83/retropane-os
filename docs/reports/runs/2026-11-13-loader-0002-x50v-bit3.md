# loader-0002 (X50v), 2026-11-13: CPLD 0x1C bit 3 set at 30 s, R193

- **Unit:** the owner's X50v (Windows Mobile 2003), HaRET and the loader
  (`06676a68`, opt-in way A) started from the CF card's `RetroPaneX50v`
  folder (`set cmdline "rp.x50v-usb-bit3"`); USB cable to the PC.
- **Card log:** `rplog/` `loader-0002` on the CF card, read on the X51v
  with `cargo xtask cardlog loader 2`; the whole log is in
  `local/cardlog/loader-0002.txt`. The PC's USB arrivals were recorded at
  the same time (`local/x50v-pc-usb-2026-11-13.txt`, PC clock).
- **Sheet:** `docs/research/sheets/r193-x50v-cpld1c-bit3.md`.
- **Verdict: bit 3 is the X50v's USB switch.** For 30 s with bit 3 off,
  as in loader-0001: 0 setups, resets only from the loader's own replugs.
  At 30.764 s bit 3 went on (`0004 -> 000C`, Windows Mobile's slot and the
  board record with it), the loader replugged at 30.920 s, the first
  SETUP came at 32.951 s, and the PC configured the device at 33.137 s
  (11 setups). The PC listed the loader's serial port
  (`USB\VID_1209&PID_0001\RP26-0001`, COM3) at 16:26:26 (PC clock). When
  the owner pulled the cable at about 2 minutes, the PC dropped it
  (16:28:19) and listed it again when he plugged it back in (16:28:32)
  with no software step on the X50v. It was dropped at his soft reset
  (16:38:50).
- **Not found:** the cable pin. The start-up log closes when the PC
  configures the device (at 33 s), so the cable pull at about 2 minutes
  wasn't logged. Before then GPIO14 (the X51v's cable pin) fell at 1.347 s
  with the cable in: it is not the X50v's cable detect.
- The layout the PC took was serial-only (the loader's fallback had
  switched to it at 26 s); the drive layout is untested on the X50v.
