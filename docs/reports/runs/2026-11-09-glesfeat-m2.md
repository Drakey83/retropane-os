**What it means:** a core reset doesn't clear a two-unit hang on the TA
path; turning the 3D clock off for a millisecond does. That was the
difference between the recovery and the next image's start-up, which
writes `MBXCLK` before its reset. The drivers now cycle the clock before
every hang recovery: the 3D renderer's, and the TA path's
`Ready::recover`, which brought the core back 3 of 3 in the second run.
