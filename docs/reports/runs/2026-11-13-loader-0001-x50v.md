# loader-0001 (X50v), 2026-11-13: the loader's start-up log, R193

- **Unit:** the owner's X50v (Windows Mobile 2003), HaRET and the loader
  (build 26, `e72f1b1b`) started from the X51v's CF card, `RetroPane`
  folder; USB cable to the PC throughout.
- **Card log:** `rplog/` `loader-0001` on the CF card (no SD card in),
  read on the X51v with `cargo xtask cardlog loader 1`; the whole log is
  kept in `local/cardlog/loader-0001.txt` (11,141 bytes).
- **What it measured:** the board as the loader found it, then every USB
  event for 5 minutes (sheet `docs/research/sheets/r193-loader-startup-log.md`).
- **Verdict:** the log works on the device (89 sector writes, 0 refused,
  0 bytes dropped, closed at 5 minutes). USB: 0 setups in 5 minutes; 34
  resets, one per loader replug plus 2 at the start. `UP2OCR` and `UDCCR`
  as on the working X51v. CPLD `0x1C` bit 3 off at every stage. Read in
  docs/hw/udc.md "The X50v".
