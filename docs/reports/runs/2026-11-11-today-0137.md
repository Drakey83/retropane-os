## The fix for (a) and (b): the frame finds where its list changed

Windows Mobile's GWES leaves damage to the caller (`InvalidateRect`, else
the whole window), which is what the shell did. Today's retained renderers
find it from the lists (Chromium's raster invalidation, Flutter's layer
diff). Ours, at this list's size: each command gets a 64-bit key (FNV-1a of
its fields, its text's bytes and its clip) and the area it may touch; the
last frame's keys are kept (8 KB, in the image's RAM pool: its zeroed RAM
budget had 1.1 KB left, and the first build exceeded it at 88,996 of
81,920 bytes); the lists are matched from both ends and only the commands
between mark their areas (`rp_draw::changes`). Data that may show anywhere
(a picture, a forecast, a radar frame's PNG, the station, the graph and
chart pictures, the units) now restates instead of marking the whole
screen; changes under the list (the wallpaper, the radar map) still mark
their own areas. ADR-0021 rule 1 (the shell owns which rectangles changed)
and rule 2 (the per-frame list; the renderer unchanged).
